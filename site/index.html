Everything Your Network Needs

Ten specialized services. One seamlessly integrated partner. Zero compromises on quality or performance.

LAN Networking
Gigabit and 10G structured local area networks with enterprise Cisco/HPE switches, VLAN segmentation, QoS and spanning tree optimization for zero-downtime performance.
Fiber Optic Networking
Single-mode and multi-mode fiber solutions from 1G to 100G. DWDM metro rings, OLT-ONU GPON networks, fusion splicing and full OTDR certification included.
Enterprise WiFi (WiFi 6/6E)
High-density wireless deployments with Cisco Meraki, Aruba or Ubiquiti UniFi platforms. Heat mapping, RF optimization, RADIUS authentication and cloud management included.
CCTV & Security Systems
IP surveillance with Hikvision/Dahua 4K cameras, NVR/DVR recording, AI analytics (people counting, license plate recognition), remote viewing apps and alarm integration.
Server Setup & Virtualization
Dell/HPE rack server configuration, VMware vSphere/ESXi/Hyper-V virtualization, SAN/NAS storage, backup solutions and DR planning for business continuity.
Firewall & Cybersecurity

Data Cabling

Router & Switch Management

Smart Office Infrastructure

IT Support Services
